Our recommendations

A few places we're happy to recommend to our guests — this list will grow over time.

Where to eat

AMAREA

Seafood restaurant and pizzeria in the heart of Otranto: fresh raw fish, seafood pasta and Mediterranean specialities, in an elegant yet welcoming setting.

Where to eat

Lu Tata N'zinu

Pizzeria and restaurant in Piazzetta Idro, near the old town: long-leavened pizza and fresh Salento seafood, gluten-free options available.

Where to eat

Ristorante La Pignata

A welcoming Apulian trattoria: catch of the day and local land-and-sea produce, blending family tradition with creative cooking.

Where to eat

Tenuta Scirocco

An organic countryside farmhouse with a pool, a traditional Salento restaurant, a wood-fired pizzeria and its own home-produced honey.

Rentals & transfers

Autoservizi S.r.l.

Self-drive car rental with unlimited mileage and insurance included, taxi and transfer service, scooter rental and guided tours by Ape Calessino.

Events in Otranto

A selection of upcoming events in town, picked and kept up to date by us.

Until 27 September 2026

Otranto in Luce

An immersive video-mapping show in the old town: historic façades come alive with light and sound, every evening from 9pm to midnight. Free entry.

20 September 2026

Otranto Antiqua

Antiques, vintage and collectables market along the Lungomare Kennedy seafront.

Sagra dell'Uva

The traditional grape-harvest festival, with local wine and food tastings.

Il Codice Visibile

Art exhibition at the Aragonese Castle: over 50 works exploring figure, word and pop culture.

31 December – 1 January

New Year's Eve & Alba dei Popoli

A New Year's Eve celebration in the main square, followed at dawn on 1 January by Otranto's traditional greeting to Italy's first sunrise — the town sits at the country's easternmost point.
